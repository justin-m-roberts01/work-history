NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE — ACME CORP
Last Updated: February 2025

== WEEK 1: SETUP ==
Day 1: Attend orientation session at 9am with HR (Building A, Room 102).
Day 1: Collect laptop and badge from IT desk (Building B, Lobby).
Day 1: Complete I-9 verification — bring two forms of ID.
Day 2: Complete all mandatory compliance training modules in the LMS (due by end of Week 2).
Day 3: Schedule 1:1 with your direct manager to align on 30/60/90-day goals.
Day 5: Submit your direct deposit information to Payroll via the HR portal.

== WEEK 2: ROLE ONBOARDING ==
Meet with at least 3 teammates for introductory calls.
Request access to all required systems through the IT Service Desk.
Review team documentation and project backlogs.
Attend your first team standup and sprint planning (if applicable).
Complete your benefits enrollment — deadline is 30 days from start date.

== KEY CONTACTS ==
HR Business Partner: hr-support@acme.com

Facilities: facilities@acme.com

== FIRST 90 DAYS ==
30-day goal: Understand team structure, tools, and 2–3 active workstreams.
60-day goal: Own at least one task or project component end-to-end.
90-day goal: Deliver a meaningful contribution and complete your first performance self-assessment.
Your manager will conduct a formal 90-day check-in to review your progress.

== IMPORTANT DEADLINES ==
Benefits enrollment: within 30 days of start date (no exceptions — missed window = wait until open enrollment).
Direct deposit setup: by end of Week 1 (to ensure first paycheck is direct deposited).
Compliance training: within 14 days of start date.
Equipment acknowledgment form: within 3 days of receiving corporate devices.
