EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK — ACME CORP
Last Updated: January 2025

== SECTION 1: TIME OFF ==
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year in their first three years.
After three years, PTO accrual increases to 20 days per year.
PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance.
Unused PTO up to 5 days may be rolled over into the following calendar year.
PTO beyond the 5-day rollover cap is forfeited on December 31st.

== SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK ==
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and HR documentation.
Remote employees must be reachable during core hours: 10am–3pm local time.
Equipment stipends for remote work: $500 one-time setup, $50/month ongoing.

== SECTION 3: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS ==
Annual performance reviews are conducted every November.
Employees rated "Exceeds Expectations" are eligible for merit increases of 4–7%.
Employees rated "Meets Expectations" are eligible for merit increases of 1–3%.
Employees rated "Below Expectations" are placed on a 60-day improvement plan.
Mid-year check-ins are optional but strongly encouraged.

== SECTION 4: BENEFITS ==
Health insurance enrollment opens every October for the following calendar year.
Dental and vision coverage is included at no cost for the employee (dependents extra).
The company 401(k) match is 50% of employee contributions up to 6% of salary.
Employees are vested in company 401(k) match after 2 years of service.
Tuition reimbursement: up to $5,000/year for pre-approved programs.

== SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT ==
Employees must not share confidential company information with outside parties.
Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR within 30 days of arising.
Harassment of any form will result in immediate investigation and potential termination.
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
