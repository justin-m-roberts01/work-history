IT SECURITY POLICY — ACME CORP
Last Updated: March 2025

== SECTION 1: PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS ==
All passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
Passwords must include uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character.
Passwords must be rotated every 90 days.
Password reuse is prohibited for the last 10 passwords.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all corporate accounts.

== SECTION 2: DATA CLASSIFICATION ==
Data is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted.
Restricted data includes PII, financial records, and employee compensation data.
Confidential data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
Restricted data may not be stored on personal devices under any circumstances.
Any data breach must be reported to the Security team within 1 hour of discovery.

== SECTION 3: DEVICE POLICY ==
All corporate laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled.
Personal devices may access corporate email only through the approved MDM profile.
USB drives are prohibited on corporate devices without explicit Security team approval.
Screen lock must activate after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 2 hours.

== SECTION 4: INCIDENT RESPONSE ==
Security incidents are classified as Critical, High, Medium, or Low severity.
Critical incidents require a response within 15 minutes and executive notification.
All incidents must be logged in the Security ticketing system within 24 hours.
Post-incident reviews are mandatory for Critical and High severity events.
Employees must never attempt to investigate a security incident themselves.

== SECTION 5: ACCEPTABLE USE ==
Corporate systems may not be used for personal business or profit-generating activities.
Access to competitor systems or data, even publicly available, requires Legal approval.
Employees may not install unapproved software on corporate devices.
All third-party software integrations must pass a Security Architecture Review.
VPN must be active when accessing corporate resources from public networks.
